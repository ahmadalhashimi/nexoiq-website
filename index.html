02 — Approach

How an Odoo project runs with me

No surprises in production: every change is understood, tested and explained before it goes live.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Sessions with your team to map processes, roles and pain points — before touching any configuration.

  2. 02

    Design & configure

    Standard Odoo features first; a clear list of what needs a custom module and why.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Custom modules with automated tests, kept in Git and deployed through Odoo.sh branches.

  4. 04

    Test on staging

    Every change tried on a copy of your data, with a step-by-step test guide for your managers.

  5. 05

    Train

    Key users and teams trained on their own daily flows, in their own language.

  6. 06

    Go live & support

    Backup and rollback plan before release, then follow-up while the team settles in.
